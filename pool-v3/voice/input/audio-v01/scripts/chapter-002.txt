Weather Notes. Chapter one. Arrival of the coastal front. Avery opened the shutters and looked toward the sea. Clouds were moving inland, while the ground near the station was still dry. The first reading was taken before breakfast. The second reading was taken after the wind changed direction. Avery recorded the measurements in the weather log, not in the geological field journal. By midday, a thin band of rain had reached the western hill. The team moved the outdoor equipment under cover and waited for the front to pass. The next chapter describes the clear evening that followed.
