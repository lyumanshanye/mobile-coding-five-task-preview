Weather Notes. Chapter two. Return of clear skies. After sunset, the rain stopped and the clouds began to separate. Avery checked the outdoor instruments and compared the evening readings with the morning log. A small pool remained beside the gate, but the eastern path was dry. The team reopened the shutters and prepared the equipment for the following day. No geological samples were moved during this inspection. The weather log stayed on the desk beside the window. Before turning off the light, Avery recorded one final observation: the coastal front had passed, and the night was calm.
