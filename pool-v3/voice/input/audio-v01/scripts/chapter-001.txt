Field Journal. Chapter two. Return through the valley. The afternoon walk began at the narrow stream. Morgan counted twelve sealed bags and checked every label against the notebook. One label was difficult to read, so the team stopped and replaced it before continuing. At the bridge they met Avery, who was carrying a separate collection from the southern basin. The collections stayed in different boxes. Back at the station, both teams compared their notes and planned the next survey. The north ridge report was complete, but the instrument calibration still needed another check. This is the end of the field journal.
