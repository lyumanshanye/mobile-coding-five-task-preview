Field Journal. Chapter one. Arrival at the north ridge. Morgan reached the field station shortly after sunrise. The road was dry, but the valley was covered in mist. Before leaving the vehicle, the team checked the sample bags, spare batteries, and the paper map. Each person wrote the date in a separate notebook. At the first outcrop, Morgan found dark basalt beside a narrow stream. No samples were collected yet. The team first photographed the exposed rock and marked the location. This chapter ends at the north ridge. The next chapter follows the return route through the valley.
